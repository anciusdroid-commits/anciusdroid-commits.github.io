ABOUT ME

Alexandru-Adrian StanciuBucharest · remote-friendly

I started in 2006 filming and cutting match analysis for professional football — the Romania National Team, Liga 1 clubs, and teams in Qatar and Greece. That's where I learned to find the story in hours of footage, fast.

Since then I've edited cinema, TV and social ads at Hogarth Worldwide, directed long- and short-form sports shows for YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, and led motion design for gaming brands. I studied Film & TV Directing at Hyperion University.

Today I run an AI-directed video pipeline built on Claude Code — custom skills and MCP connectors that automate reframing, export plans and broadcast QC — and use it to ship campaigns like the World Cup 2026 hero film as a one-person delivery.
